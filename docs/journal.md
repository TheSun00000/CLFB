## 2026-11-01 (evening, autonomous) - fast eps-only retention study
User asked for a much faster test: fine-tune on one task for 30k updates, no rollouts, and look at eps of the 8 tasks only (waves e1-e7, ~270 runs in long multi-command jobs, `tools/queue_eps.py`, report `tools/eps_report.py`, plan and log `docs/eps_plan.md`). Findings: (1) eps of the trained task is at its floor after 10k updates; without retention terms the eps of the other tasks rises (+0.08 to +0.16); (2) mu_q 0.5 (alone or with mu_b 100) removes it on all 8 tasks (3 seeds), for +0.01-0.02 of eps on the trained task; (3) in a stream the default alpha 0.5 does not keep earlier tasks (and the stored latents explode); alpha 0.99 does, and mu_b 300 keeps most of the gain over an 8-task stream, with valid stored latents; ablations: mu_b is what holds the learned tasks, mu_q the unlearned ones. Recommended: alpha 0.99, mu_b 300, mu_q 0.5 (ridge 0, est_same 1). Returns not checked. Tracker: new family 'eps-study' (3 charts per row, stream tables), page and jobs sharding changed (see CLAUDE.md).
