Overview

Reward encoding in a pretrained forward-backward (FB) model, one task after another, at fixed rank.

Question

An FB model represents a family of rewards through a latent z and a backward embedding B. When new reward tasks arrive in a stream, can the model keep encoding them, and keep the latents of earlier tasks valid, without growing the rank or forgetting?

Setup
model
classical FB (actor in the bootstrap) + DDPG actor, loaded from a pretrained checkpoint
stream
ordered eval tasks of one environment, for example walker: walk, then run
stage k
one task; steps_per_stage updates against a frozen θ_old = θ_(k-1)
data
full offline dataset, plus a labeled set D_k of 10k states for the current task
Environments
cheetah
vel10Back, vel2Back, vel2, vel10, flip
walker
stand, walk, vel8, run, flip
quadruped
stand, walk, run, jump
maze
pointmaze and antmaze goal tasks
Stage objective
L_k = L_FB + λ · L_rec(r_k) + μ_B · R^B + μ_Q · R^Q

L_rec

Regression of the normalized reward on B_θ over the labeled set, with detached coefficients. Optional cross-fitting.

R^B

Retention in latent space. Closed form of E_(z~ν)[ zᵀMz / zᵀΣ_old⁻¹z ] with M = Σ_old⁻¹ − TᵀΣ_θ⁻¹T and T = AΣ_old⁻¹.

R^Q

Value matching between the old and new model on the re-encoded old family, z' = sg(Tz), head by head.

ν mixes the uniform sphere with the stored atoms z̄_j (weight α). At the end of a stage the stored latents are carried forward, z_j ← T z_j, and the new one is z_k = mean_i r̄_k(s_i) B_θk(s_i). Only d numbers per task are kept.

Hyperparameters
lam
weight of L_rec (1.0)
mu_b, mu_q
weights of R^B (1.0) and R^Q (0.01)
alpha
atom weight in ν (0.5)
n_labeled
size of D_k (10000)
steps_per_stage
updates per stage (100000)
bc_coef
TD3+BC weight of the actor (0)
Metrics logged
  • fresh: zero-shot, z inferred from fresh labels
  • tracked: z carried through the T's
  • eps: share of reward energy not explained by B on held-out states
  • z_shrink: mean ‖Tz‖ / ‖z‖
  • rew_corr: correlation of the encoded and true reward
